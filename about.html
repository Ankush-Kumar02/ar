About Our School

A Legacy of Excellence in Education

Our Story

Founded with a vision to transform education, A.R. Agarwal International School has been a beacon of excellence for over 30 years.

Our journey began with a simple yet powerful mission: to provide every child with an education that nurtures their mind, body, and spirit. Today, we stand as one of the leading educational institutions in the region, known for our academic excellence and holistic approach to learning.

With state-of-the-art infrastructure, experienced faculty, and a curriculum designed to inspire curiosity, we prepare our students to be responsible global citizens and leaders of tomorrow.
