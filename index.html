About Our School

A.R. Agarwal International School is committed to providing quality education that prepares students for the challenges of tomorrow.

With over 30 years of experience in the field of education, we have consistently produced outstanding results. Our dedicated faculty, state-of-the-art infrastructure, and student-centric approach make us one of the best schools in the region.

We believe in nurturing not just academic excellence, but also character building, leadership skills, and overall personality development.
